Purpose of this page

This page accompanies the manuscript during peer review. It provides the material for checking the reported numbers: the measurements behind Tables 3 to 6 and a script that recomputes the tables from them.

The manuscript defines the method, the rows of the tables and the experimental protocol. They are not repeated here, and the files label rows and arms by their order in the manuscript.

How to run

The script needs Python 3 with NumPy and SciPy and runs in a few seconds. It was tested with Python 3.12, NumPy 1.26.4 and SciPy 1.11.4 and 1.13.1.

git clone https://github.com/panzerjagerWang/RIFT-Map.git
cd RIFT-Map/reproduce
pip install -r requirements.txt
python reproduce_tables.py

It prints every recomputed entry beside the value printed in the manuscript and ends with a summary. With --quiet it prints the summary alone:

table3    4 of 4 entries equal the manuscript
table4    11 of 11 entries equal the manuscript
text_5_3  5 of 5 entries equal the manuscript
table5    7 of 7 entries equal the manuscript
table6    9 of 9 entries equal the manuscript
text_5_5  3 of 3 entries equal the manuscript
ALL VALUES REPRODUCED

The exit status is 0 when every value equals the manuscript and 1 otherwise. The bootstrap uses a fixed seed, so the intervals are reproduced to the printed digit.

What is recomputed

In the manuscriptRecomputed quantitiesInput in reproduce/data/
Table 3
Registration of the middle-camera frames		registered frames, number of connected models and size of the largest model, from the model sizes of each runtable3_registration.csv
Table 4
Discrepancy of the 31 withheld intervals		MAE, RMSE, 95% bootstrap interval of the RMSE (20,000 resamples), largest discrepancy, one-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test and the number of intervals with a smaller absolute discrepancy, from the signed discrepancy of every interval under each of the eleven rowstable4_withheld_intervals.csv
Table 5
Agreement of the renderings with six withheld photographs		image coverage and structural similarity per frame and surface, their means and the share of tape points inside the tape masktable5_rendering_agreement.csv
Table 6
Simulation results over 100 seeds		mean and standard deviation of the scale error, camera error, MAE and RMSE of every tape and arm, from the result of every seedtable6_simulation.csv
Sections 5.3 and 5.5
Numbers quoted in the text		the ranges and counts that the text states for single rows, intervals and seeds (eight entries)the files of Tables 4 and 6

Rows are numbered as the manuscript prints them: row01 is the first row of Table 4, tape1 and arm1 the first tape and the first arm of Table 6.

Not part of this release

The reconstruction software, the survey images and the reconstructed scene are not part of this release. Reviewers who need them for their assessment can request them through the handling editor.
